Privacy Policy — Claude Calendar MCP

Last updated: 2026-10-26

What data is accessed

With your consent, the app accesses your Google Calendar data (calendars and events) through the Google Calendar API, only to show and change events at your own request.

Where data is stored

OAuth tokens and calendar data stay on your own computer. The app has no server and does not send your data to the app's author or any third party.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the locally stored tokens.
